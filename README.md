📘 **Project Description: Web Performance Analysis**
This project focuses on analyzing website performance metrics to understand user behavior, marketing channel effectiveness, and engagement trends over time. The data used was sourced from web analytics (e.g., Google Analytics) and includes detailed session and user-level information across various acquisition channels.

🛠️ **Objectives:**
To evaluate how different marketing channels contribute to website traffic.

To identify engagement patterns across time and channels.

To assess which channels are most effective in retaining user attention.

To provide actionable insights for improving underperforming channels.

📊 **Data Processing & Analysis:**
The dataset was cleaned and formatted, with date-time values parsed for time-based analysis.

Numeric metrics such as sessions, users, engagement rate, and average engagement time were standardized for accurate comparison.

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) was conducted using pandas, matplotlib, and seaborn to uncover trends and channel-specific behaviors.

📌 **Key Insights:**
User & Session Trends: A noticeable spike in both users and sessions was observed between May 16–20, 2024, suggesting increased marketing activity or campaign success during that period.

Top Traffic Source: Organic Social emerged as the highest contributor of users (~48,000), making it the most effective channel for driving traffic.

Content Effectiveness: Organic Videos recorded the highest average engagement time (>3 minutes), indicating high content value and retention potential. Email, while limited in traffic, maintained solid engagement.

Engagement Rate Leader: The Referral channel stood out with the highest engagement rate (0.7), signaling high-quality traffic from external sources.

Engaged vs Non-Engaged Sessions: Organic Social showed a balanced ratio, while Direct traffic had significantly more non-engaged sessions, pointing to possible UX or intent mismatch.

Peak Traffic Timing: Organic Social traffic peaked at 12 AM, revealing insights into user activity cycles.

Engagement Correlation: There’s no direct correlation between high traffic and high engagement—emphasizing the need for quality content over quantity.

📈 **Conclusion:**
This analysis provides actionable recommendations for improving marketing performance:

Leverage successful tactics from Organic Social across other channels.

Focus on improving engagement for Direct and Email traffic with personalized content and UX enhancements.

Optimize content publishing based on peak activity times (e.g., midnight).

Use high-engagement channels like Organic Video and Referral for deeper funnel strategies.
